1

Define the parking zone

Use the near-side kerb in front of the right-hand wall and to its right. Include any space to the left needed for a left-facing car’s front.

For gap estimates, mark four corners of a ground-plane rectangle. Start with the two ends of the far edge, along the kerb, then the near edge in reverse order.

2

Use an image as the reference

Mark the two ends of a gap you know fits, or your parked car’s length projected onto the road. No make or model needed.

A known-fit gap already includes room to manoeuvre. Add a margin when using your car’s footprint.

Use Rear limits to mark where the rear can sit within cable reach: just right of the bin to the furthest reachable point. Use Ideal position to favour a car centred in front of the right-hand wall. Place these marks on the road, not on the bin or wall.
